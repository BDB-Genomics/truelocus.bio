Resolving Distal Target Genes for Non-Coding CRISPR Screens Without Cell-Type Chromatin Conformation

High-throughput CRISPR screens identify functionally critical non-coding enhancers, yet assigning causal target genes remains confounded by 3D chromatin looping and skip-gene configurations. Here, we present TrueLocus, an in silico architecture that accurately pairs non-coding screen hits to true causal genes purely from primary sequence.
