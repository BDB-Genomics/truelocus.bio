Methodological Notes • Failure Forensics

Two Engine Failures on Real Data (And What They Taught Us)

A model that documents where it fails is more useful than one that claims it doesn't. In our 72-pair held-out CRISPRi benchmark, our engine missed twice. Both failures were ours.

Failure 1 • HBE1 (Annotation Error)
An annotation error, not biology.

The database placed HBE1's transcription start 225 kb from its real promoter. The CAGE peak showed the true promoter directly, but one read-through transcript model moved the gene 225 kb. Six loci flipped rank.

Takeaway: Anchor promoters to measured CAGE peaks, not transcript models.
Failure 2 • NCR3LG1 (Data Dropout)
A data dropout that looked like silence.

One CAGE library returned 0 TPM, so our engine treated the gene as silent. Three distant competitors outranked it. Yet TAP-seq in the same cell line showed it was strongly expressed (p = 3.7 × 10-104).

Takeaway: Never gate promoter competence on a single data source.
Root Cause & Design Fix: Cheap inputs trusted too hard. Both failures treated a data-layer artifact as ground truth. This is why our audits request your own cell-type ATAC and RNA data—measured signals beat assumptions.
Case Study • Mechanistic Failure Forensic

The 1p13 LDL Locus: Where Distance Priors Break on Gain-of-Function Mutations

On the landmark 1p13 cholesterol locus (Musunuru et al., Nature 2010; PMID 20686566), the causal variant rs12740374 creates a de novo C/EBP binding site. Because the proximal promoter PSRC1 is physically 13.5× closer (9.1 kb vs 123 kb) and active in adult liver, TrueLocus v1.8 distance decay ranks PSRC1 #1 (42.2% share) and depresses causal SORT1 to #6 (4.8% share).

Evaluated on TrueLocus v1.8 continuous scaling architecture. Expression receipts pulled from GTEx Portal API v2 (gtex_v10 adult liver, 100+ donors).
•
The Multi-eQTL Reality: In human liver biopsies, rs12740374 upregulates both PSRC1 and SORT1 (>12-fold). The variant physically transactivates both promoters.
•
Distance Dominance: Because PSRC1 is already competent (1.86 TPM) and 9.1 kb away, distance decay gives it a 9.0× contact advantage over SORT1 at 123 kb.
•
Phenotypic Decoupling: Musunuru et al. proved in mice that Psrc1 overexpression has zero effect on plasma lipids, whereas Sort1 drives a >2-fold functional shift in LDL-C and VLDL.
Takeaway for v2.0: Solving 1p13 with Biophysics

Statistical distance-decay priors (d−1.0) inherently break when an intervening bystander promoter is also transcriptionally active. The upcoming TrueLocus v2.0 biophysical engine is engineered to resolve this class of gain-of-function loci through three physical layers:

  • Allele-Specific Binding Energy (ΔΔG): Modeling Poisson-Boltzmann minor-groove electrostatics to quantify de novo C/EBP binding affinity created by the G>T point mutation.
  • Enhancer–Promoter Stereochemical Compatibility: Evaluating transcription factor co-activator matching between the enhancer complex and SORT1's specific core promoter architecture.
  • Sequence-Dependent Polymer Bending: Calculating persistence length (Lp) and helical phasing along intervening DNA to evaluate intrinsic looping strain across the 123 kb span.
