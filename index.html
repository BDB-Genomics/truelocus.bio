The Unresolved Regulatory Loop

High-throughput CRISPRi screens identify functional non-coding coordinates, but target gene assignment remains ambiguous. Defaulting to the nearest transcription start site (TSS) fails when distal enhancers loop past proximal genes. In held-out CRISPRi validation sets (Schraivogel et al. 2020), the true target was not the nearest gene in 45.8% of pairs (33/72).
