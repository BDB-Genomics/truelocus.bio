The Challenge of Non-Coding Targets

CRISPR screens find crucial non-coding regulatory elements, but identifying the exact gene they regulate is challenging. Simply picking the nearest gene often fails because DNA loops across megabase distances in 3D space. In tested datasets, the correct target was NOT the nearest gene ~50% of the time.

Conventional Path
Months of Wasted Effort
Testing incorrect nearby genes based on linear 1D distance alone wastes months of bench validation and millions in discovery capital.
