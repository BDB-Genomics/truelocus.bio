02 • Problem Space

The Challenge of Non-Coding Targets

CRISPR screens find crucial non-coding regulatory elements, but identifying the exact gene they regulate is challenging. Simply picking the nearest gene often fails because DNA loops across megabase distances in 3D space. In tested datasets, the correct target was not the nearest gene nearly 50% of the time.

Conventional Approach

Months of Wasted Validation

Testing incorrect nearby genes based on linear 1D distance alone wastes months of bench validation and millions in discovery capital.

TrueLocus Engine

Zero Cell-Type Hi-C Required

Deploys sequence-derived 3D chromatin architecture models to pinpoint true causal target genes directly from primary sequence before in vivo commitment.
