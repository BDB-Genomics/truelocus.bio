The Unresolved Regulatory Loop

When you complete a high-throughput CRISPRi screen, non-coding hit coordinates are clear, but target gene assignment is not. Conventional pipelines default to assigning each hit to its nearest transcription start site (TSS). In held-out CRISPRi benchmarks (Schraivogel et al. 2020), the true target was not the nearest gene in 45.8% of pairs (33/72). A screening hit identifies a functional coordinate, but leaves the causal target gene an open, unresolved liability heading into animal validation.

Clear scope boundaries where 3D target de-risking is unnecessary:
