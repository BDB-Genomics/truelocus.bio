The Unresolved Regulatory Loop

When you complete a high-throughput CRISPRi screen, non-coding hit coordinates are clear, but target gene assignment is not. Conventional pipelines default to assigning each hit to its nearest transcription start site (TSS). In held-out CRISPRi benchmarks (Schraivogel et al. 2020), the true target was not the nearest gene in 45.8% of pairs (33/72). A screening hit identifies a functional coordinate, but leaves the causal target gene an open, unresolved liability heading into animal validation.

Objective Evaluation & Domains of Applicability:
  • Broad ABC Model (Nasser et al. 2021): A foundational milestone in regulatory genomics. When deeply sequenced cell-type Hi-C, ChIP-seq, and ATAC-seq are experimentally available, Broad ABC demonstrates outstanding performance (75.0% Top-1, 94.4% Top-3 recall). It remains the benchmark standard for data-rich cell lines.
  • Nearest-TSS Heuristic: An indispensable, zero-overhead baseline. In gene-dense regions where enhancers regulate their immediate adjacent promoter, 1D proximity correctly assigns 54.2% of targets.
  • TrueLocus Engine: Engineered specifically for screening hits in primary cells and disease models where experimental cell-type Hi-C is absent or impractical to generate. Via sequence-derived contact priors and multi-promoter competition resolution, TrueLocus lifts Top-3 recall on non-nearest loci to 70% (vs 42% for Nearest-TSS), correctly ranking the true target #1 on 13 of these 33 loci where nearest-gene picked the wrong gene, without requiring cell-type Hi-C.
